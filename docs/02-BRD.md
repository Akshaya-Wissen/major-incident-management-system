# Business Requirements Document

## Business Goal

Provide a clear incident command workflow for major availability events so stakeholders can see ownership, impact, mitigation progress, RCA quality, and closure readiness.

## Business Requirements

- Track every major incident from detection through closure.
- Preserve a timeline of decisions and communications.
- Capture customer and business impact before delegation.
- Require resolution details before RCA.
- Require approved RCA before closure.
- Maintain reusable incident response knowledge.

## Success Criteria

- A reviewer can demonstrate the complete lifecycle with seeded data.
- New incidents can be opened without user accounts or access management.
- The UI clearly separates active response, RCA, and knowledge base workflows.
